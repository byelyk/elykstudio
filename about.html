A social-first creative agency

We don't change your brand.
We change the room it walks into.

“If I put this candle in an all-white gallery space, it looks like a piece of art. If I put this candle in a garage, it looks like a piece of trash. I can either spend my time designing the candle, or I can spend my time designing the room that it sits in.”
— Virgil Abloh

We think about brands the same way. It's not always about changing yourself or your product — sometimes you just have to change the way it's delivered. It's not about being perfect. It's about being in the right room, in front of the people who actually resonate with it.

That's what we do. We take what a brand already has and make it even better — then we put it in the rooms where it belongs, so the message reaches further and lands with the people who were always meant to hear it.

Why we exist

We get brands and creators
in the same room.

For brands

You don't need the biggest audience. You need the right one. We put your product in front of people who actually care about it — which is why our work converts instead of just collecting views. And you leave with content you own and can repurpose on your own channels, saving you the budget and the months it would take to make it yourself.

For creators

Talent shouldn't have to wait to be discovered. We put creators in front of the brands that fit them — real partnerships with real budgets, not one-off posts. The goal is a relationship that pays off for years, not a single check.

When both sides win, the partnership lasts. That's the whole model — honest numbers, honest expectations, and a return on investment both ends can point to.

The founder

Kyle Okoth

Founder · Content Creator

Elyk Studio is creator-led, and that's the point. I'm a content creator first — I've spent years building an audience, sitting on the other side of brand deals, and learning what actually makes a partnership work versus what wastes everyone's money.

Along the way I got put on some big stages — partnerships with companies like Microsoft, Timberland, Adobe and Best Buy, and rooms most creators never get invited into. I know how much of that came down to access. So I built Elyk Studio to hand that access to other people: creators with real talent who haven't been found yet, and companies with genuinely good products who don't know these rooms exist.

I run this the way I'd want to be treated as a creator — clear numbers, honest reporting, and no overselling. If a campaign isn't the right fit, I'll say so. The relationships are worth more than any single deal.

Let's get you in the right room.
